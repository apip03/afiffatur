Project Overview

An industrial-grade analog signal conditioning and automation training testbed engineered as part of Case Based Learning (CBL) Dasar Otomasi Industri 2 at ITS. The integrated system packages a dual adjustable power supply (±12V), BJT transistor common-emitter amplification/switching stages, an operational amplifier (LM358) zero-span signal conditioning converter for standard 4–20 mA industrial current loops, and a PWM-controlled DC motor driver into a standardized testbench enclosure box equipped with banana jacks and digital monitoring displays.

My Role & Responsibilities

Served as the Team Leader overseeing full project lifecycle execution, while directly spearheading transistor and operational amplifier hardware engineering:

  • Team Leadership & Project Management: Led a multidisciplinary team of 7 engineering students; orchestrated project roadmap, delegated hardware sub-tasks, scheduled lab testing sessions, and managed overall project milestones.
  • Transistor Circuit Analysis, Simulation & Realization: Formulated theoretical calculations, conducted circuit simulations, and hand-fabricated the BJT transistor analysis board (point-to-point PCB wiring) to examine base-collector current amplification ($I_B, I_C$) and collector-emitter voltage ($V_{CE}$) operating curves.
  • Op-Amp Zero-Span Signal Conditioning: Co-designed, wired, and validated the operational amplifier circuit (LM358) implementing zero-span calibration to convert analog input voltages (0.5V – 5.0V) into standardized industrial current signals (4 mA – 20 mA).
  • Budgeting & Cost Estimation (RAB): Developed the Bill of Materials (BOM) and managed the project expenditure budget (Rencana Anggaran Biaya) for all enclosure components, dual-rail power supplies, banana binding posts, and semiconductor parts.
  • Collaborative Technical Reporting & Error Analysis: Co-authored and reviewed the final engineering report, consolidating circuit schematics, mathematical models, empirical multi-variable measurement data, and discrepancy error analysis.

Key Features & Engineering Outcomes

4–20 mA Industrial Current Loop

Calibrated Op-Amp Zero-Span circuit mapping 0.5V–5.0V inputs to 4.01mA–19.57mA outputs for industrial transmitter compatibility.

BJT Transistor Characterization

Rigorous empirical testing of $I_B$, $I_C$, and $V_{CE}$ across multiple resistance steps with complete error discrepancy analysis.

Modular Enclosure Box

Custom-built white enclosure featuring dual digital voltmeters, banana jack patch terminals, and isolated sub-circuit boards.

End-to-End Team Leadership

Successfully led a 7-member team, completing budgeting, hardware fabrication, and technical reporting on schedule.
