Project Overview

An integrated modular learning testbed developed as part of Case Based Learning (CBL) Dasar Otomasi 1 at the Department of Electrical Automation Engineering, ITS. The project encompasses fundamental electronic and automation building blocks mounted on an acrylic platform, including an AC-to-DC regulated power supply, voltage and current divider circuits, RC transient charging/discharging networks, digital counter and 2-bit adder logic with 7-segment displays, and a DC motor control unit.

My Role & Responsibilities

Responsible for end-to-end hardware prototyping, theoretical mathematical calculations, comprehensive data acquisition, and project cost budgeting:

  • Theoretical Calculations & Circuit Design: Formulated mathematical models and calculated component parameters for voltage divider, current divider, and RC transient circuits to meet design specifications and charging/discharging time constants.
  • Hardware Realization & Soldering: Fabricated, wired, and hand-soldered the analog divider and RC filter modules on perfboards (dot-matrix PCB), ensuring solid solder joints, circuit isolation, and clean integration onto the master acrylic testbed.
  • Comprehensive Data Acquisition: Conducted experimental measurement and testing across all sub-circuits using digital multimeters and oscilloscopes to acquire empirical data and compare actual output vs. theoretical calculations.
  • Cost Estimation & Budgeting (RAB): Developed the Bill of Materials (BOM) and structured the project budget (Rencana Anggaran Biaya) to optimize component procurement and cost efficiency.
  • Technical Report & Documentation: Authored the comprehensive final engineering report, documenting schematic diagrams, mathematical derivations, component datasheets, and experimental data analysis.

Key Features & Engineering Outcomes

Precise Voltage & Current Dividers

Rigorous resistor sizing resulting in stable regulated divider outputs matching theoretical targets within tight tolerance margins.

RC Transient Response Analysis

Experimental validation of capacitor charging and discharging curves (time constant τ = R·C) verified via signal measurements.

Modular Hardware Integration

Centralized testbed combining regulated power supply, analog dividers, digital logic (counter/adder), and DC motor control on an acrylic base.

100% Data Verification & Budget Accuracy

Comprehensive dataset collected for all modules alongside structured BOM costing executed within allocated budget.
