Project Overview

Two conversion projects in one semester. First, a 25 HP diesel boat tractor (Agropro FS-28E) for muddy paddy fields was converted to a 10 kW PMSM drive with a Votol EM350 controller, a 72 V 140 Ah LiFePO₄ battery and a real-time CAN Bus monitoring dashboard. Second, a previously converted Honda Vario that had failed was repaired and re-integrated with a 2 kW BLDC mid-drive, a Votol EM-100 controller, a 72 V 20 Ah battery and a Ready-to-Drive safety system.

The tractor was field-tested ploughing a muddy paddy field without recharging. The motorcycle was tested on the road and on a dyno with three rider loads (51, 83 and 107 kg).

My Role

Processed and analysed the tractor test data (battery voltage, SOC, current, temperature, efficiency); integrated CAN Bus communication between the motor controller, BMS and the ESP32 + Nextion dashboard; designed and installed the electrical wiring of the converted motorcycle.

Key Features & Results

170 Nm Peak Torque

Up from 84 Nm on the original diesel engine, giving high torque at low speed for muddy fields.

3.37 h Continuous Operation

Field test without recharging (target: more than 3 h), covering 4.38 km and about 1,972 m².

94.6% System Efficiency

Average efficiency of the electric drivetrain measured during the field test.

42-52 km Range per Charge

Two-wheeler EV range across 51-107 kg rider loads (target: at least 30 km), with 52-57 km/h top speed.
